How to read this. Two surfaces, a few directions each. The Selected / Shipped green pill is the chosen direction — the others are alternates. Wallet options differ only in the header treatment and whether rows group by day; all use the same colour-coded icon rows. Schedule options trade off card richness vs. list density; the shipped card and the compact list can coexist (card grid in the Sports hub, list in the aside).

Decided: Wallet W2 + Schedule S1. The schedule board already ships S1. design-catalog.html now shows the wallet ledger as the W2 card; wiring W2 into LeagueActivity is the remaining step. Sample teams/amounts are illustrative; every value shown maps to a real field.
