Rail & Chip

Keep the carded look the app already has, but make it disciplined. Every surface is a card on one radius scale; state is always the same 3px left rail (never a tinted border, a bg wash, or a coloured text — those all collapse into the rail); every list is the same lead · body · trail row; chips are one family with three roles. The lowest-risk path — closest to what ships today, just consistent.

What standardizes. One .row primitive replaces ~8 inline flex layouts. The rail is the single state channel — bet outcome, live, leader, action all read the same way and nothing else colours a border or background. Chips are one family (category / line / status), so the schedule "odds" chip and a league's "head-to-head" chip finally match. The last <table> becomes rows like everything else. Lowest effort, highest familiarity — but the app stays visually "boxy".
