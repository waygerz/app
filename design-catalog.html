Representative renders of the live components — content is illustrative, structure and palette match the app's dark theme. Accent language: primary violet (actions, H2H), brand green (wins), destructive red (losses), amber (pick'em / digest), sky (friends), fuchsia (invites). The two real <table>s are the wallet ledger and the schedule board; everything labelled "standings/leaderboard" is a card list. Open this file in a browser to browse.
