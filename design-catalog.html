Representative renders of the live components — content is illustrative, structure and palette match the app's dark theme. Accent language: primary violet (actions, H2H), brand green (wins), destructive red (losses), amber (pick'em / digest), sky (friends), fuchsia (invites). The wallet ledger is now a card (statement strip + colour-coded feed); the schedule board is the last real <table> shown here; everything labelled "standings/leaderboard" is a card list.
