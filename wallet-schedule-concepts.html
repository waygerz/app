Waygerz · component redesigns

Wallet ledger & Schedule board

These two are the only raw <table>s left in the app — every other surface is a card, so on a phone they read as unfinished and cramped. Below are three directions that fold both into the card system. Each direction shows the ledger and the board together so they feel like one family. Same slate palette and primitives as the live app.

Direction 01

Icon rows & matchup cards

The lightest lift. The ledger becomes a list of rows inside a card — an icon chip that colour-codes the transaction type, the label, and the amount + running balance right-aligned. The schedule becomes a matchup card per game: the two teams stacked with logos, three clearly-labelled tappable markets. Reads like the notifications sheet and the bet cards you already have.

Direction 02

Balance hero & date timeline

Answers the first question a user actually has — "what's my balance and am I up or down?" — before the detail. A hero band shows the balance, the week's net, and a tiny bar sparkline; the rows below are grouped by day with a per-day net. The schedule gets the same hero treatment: a slate header (date + game count) over matchup rows, so a whole day's card reads at a glance.

Direction 03

Pro statement & sportsbook board

Keeps the tabular density for power users but dresses it in card chrome — rounded card, slate header row, hover highlight, a leading type icon, mono amounts, and a running-total footer. The schedule becomes a true "board": one row per game across the slate, team names left, each market a tidy two-option away/home stack — the sportsbook layout people already recognise, but on-brand.

How to read these. Direction 01 is the smallest change and the most consistent with the existing cards — best if you want the catalog to feel uniform fast. Direction 02 is the most user-friendly for the wallet specifically (balance-first) and the friendliest schedule for casual players. Direction 03 keeps the most information per screen and will scale best to a long history or a full slate, at the cost of being denser on a phone. They're mix-and-matchable — e.g. D2's balance hero on top of D3's statement, or D1 cards for the schedule with D3's footer for the wallet. All markets are tappable (green picked state on select).
