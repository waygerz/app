Quiet Surfaces

Fewer boxes. Related items live inside one soft panel separated by hairline dividers — not one bordered card each. State is a small status dot and the colour of the number; there are no rails, no tinted backgrounds, no bordered chips. Bigger type, more air. Reads calm and modern, and long lists (feed, ledger, standings) stop looking like stacked receipts.

What standardizes. The unit of layout stops being "a card" and becomes "a panel of rows", so a league list, the open-bets list, the schedule and the ledger are the same object. State drops from five encodings to one dot + the sign/colour of the number. Chips mostly disappear — category becomes coloured text, odds become a trailing mono line. Calmest and most scalable; the trade-off is less visual "pop" per item, so hero moments (invite previews, an incoming challenge) may need a louder exception.
